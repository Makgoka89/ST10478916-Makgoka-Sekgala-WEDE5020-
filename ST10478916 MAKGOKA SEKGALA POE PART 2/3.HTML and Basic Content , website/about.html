About Urban Spark Electrical

Urban Spark Electrical is a family-owned business that has serviced the Johannesburg metropolitan area for over 15 years.

The company was founded by master electrician Johan van der Merwe and has grown from a one-person operation into a team of 7 qualified electricians.

Our Mission

To provide safe, reliable and professional electrical services to homes and businesses, ensuring the safety and satisfaction of our clients.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and recommended electrical service provider in Johannesburg, known for our integrity and technical excellence.
